# UX review, 3 October 2026

The independent automated review used a fresh browser and no project history or memory. It inspected the normal entry page, all five main routes, a settled cycle and a refunded cycle on the existing local preview. Review mode was read-only; wallet signing, submissions and live announcements were outside its verified coverage. This is not a study with human participants.

## Correction plan, round 1

Recorded before implementation. Source: [initial independent report](evidence/ux-independent-1.json), cycle `6d271775-15c1-4e4f-951d-7463f44febd9`.

1. **F1, accepted:** the closed mobile sidebar still receives keyboard focus off screen. Remove the closed panel from focus and accessibility navigation. Opening and closing must preserve predictable focus, including Escape and navigation. Check Tab and Shift+Tab with the menu closed, open-menu keyboard use, focus restoration and desktop navigation.
2. **F2, accepted:** the deadline caveat has approximately 4.15:1 contrast at 9 px. Increase contrast to at least 4.5:1 and use a more readable size. Measure computed colors and verify a narrow screen without clipping.
3. **F3, accepted:** explain settlement, surplus ownership, account deposit reimbursement and decimal places in plain Polish, with equivalent English text. Preserve the exact original-owner destination and original rent-payer reimbursement rules. Check both language versions, the manual form and the cycle operations description.
4. **F4, accepted:** a refunded cycle labels a participant without a deposit as waiting and still presents an active-deposit warning. Use the program state for terminal outcomes, distinguish an absent deposit from an active wait, and retain the distinction between the browser clock and the network clock. Check Funding before/after the displayed deadline, Settled and Refunded. Preserve all transaction availability and authorization guards.

No speculative redesign is accepted. First-impression questions about matching terminology are hypotheses without a demonstrated failure; they do not justify changing the product or its financial rules. No claims of full accessibility compliance or user research will be made.

Acceptance: computed contrast at 390 and 1440 px, complete text without overlaps, and no document overflow in PL/EN.
